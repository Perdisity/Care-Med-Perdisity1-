tune ATRIMS-CareMed Business Rules

These are the owner-configurable rules required by the thesis. Changes apply to new transactions only; historical records keep the values used at the time.

Rental Deposit (FR02 / RULE-03)

Applied automatically to Delivery rentals. Walk-in rentals default to zero and may be adjusted by staff. Distance is still recorded on every delivery transaction.

Prime Gas supplier reconciliation dates (fixed by the study).

Late Return Fee (RULE-09)

Calculated on return as: days overdue × rate × units returned. Only an Administrator may override the computed amount, and every override is written to the audit log.

Damage Fee Schedule (RULE-10)

Percentage of the held deposit charged when equipment is returned damaged. Total loss is settled at the item's cash price, consistent with the rental agreement.

Low-Stock Threshold (FR07 / RULE-05)

Default reorder level for new products. Each product can override this value in its Add/Edit form, and low-stock alerts use the product's own level.
